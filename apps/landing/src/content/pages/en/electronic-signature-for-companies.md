> **Does your company need an electronic signature?** Almost certainly: contracts, public-procurement bids (SERCOP), SRI paperwork, foreign trade (ECUAPASS) and e-invoicing all require digital certificates in Ecuador. A company can obtain **two kinds of certificate**: the **legal representative** one (signs *on behalf of* the company) and the **company member** one (signs as an authorised employee). With either, you can [sign PDFs for free on firmar.ec](/en/how-to-sign-pdf/).

## The two company certificates

| Certificate | Who uses it | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| **Legal representative** (legal entity) | The registered general manager / legal representative | That the signature binds the **company**: the certificate links the person to the RUC and the current appointment. |
| **Company member** | Authorised employees | That the person signs **in their role** inside the organisation (e.g. HR lead, accountant), without legally representing it. |

A natural person with a RUC (professional, freelancer) usually needs neither — the **natural person** certificate is enough.

## What companies use it for

- **Contracts and minutes**: commercial and employment contracts, NDAs, addendums, board minutes — as PDFs with a [PAdES](/en/what-is-pades-signature/) signature, valid under Ecuador's LCE 2002-67.
- **SRI**: e-invoicing signs the XML receipts with the same certificate ([details here](/en/electronic-signature-sri-invoicing/)); administrative PDFs are signed too.
- **Public procurement (SERCOP)** and **foreign trade (ECUAPASS/SENAE)** require digital certificates.
- **HR**: onboarding and contracts signed without printing, with [free verification](/en/verify-pdf-signature/) for the employee.

## Typical requirements

They vary by ECI (ARCOTEL-accredited certification entity), but in general:

- **Legal representative**: company RUC, **current appointment** registered in the Mercantile Registry, deed of incorporation, representative's ID.
- **Company member**: the above plus an **authorisation signed by the legal representative** and the employee's ID.
- **SAS**: same as any company — a Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada has a RUC and a legal representative from incorporation, so it can obtain both certificates from day one.

## FAQ

**Can the manager sign with a personal certificate?** They can, but the signature binds them as an individual. For the document to formally commit the company and its RUC, use the **legal representative** certificate.

**How many certificates does a company need?** At least one (legal representative). If more people sign operational documents often (HR, accounting, purchasing), one **company member** certificate each avoids bottlenecks.

**Does one certificate work for SERCOP, SRI and contracts?** Yes. The certificate is one; what changes is the signature format per procedure ([XAdES for SRI receipts, PAdES for PDFs](/en/electronic-signature-sri-invoicing/)).

**What if the legal representative changes?** That certificate becomes invalid in practice (the appointment is no longer current) and must be **revoked and re-issued** for the new representative.
