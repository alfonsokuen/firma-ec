---
question: "How do you electronically sign a document?"
lang: en
order: 14
tags: [usage, signing]
---

In three steps: upload the PDF document, load your `.p12` certificate with its password and download the signed document. Everything happens inside your browser, with nothing to install and without your private key ever leaving your device. The resulting signature is PAdES, valid before the SRI, banks and public institutions. See the full guide at [Sign documents online](/en/sign-documents-online/).
