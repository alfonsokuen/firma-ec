---
question: "Can I sign documents with a Security Data certificate?"
lang: en
order: 12
tags: [compatibility, security-data]
---

Yes. firmar.ec recognises the Security Data roots (one of the most used ECIs in Ecuador) embedded in its trust list. To sign documents with your Security Data certificate, just upload it like any other `.p12` file and the app validates its trust chain offline. The same applies to certificates from BCE, ANFAC, Uanataca, Consejo de la Judicatura and other ARCOTEL-accredited entities.
