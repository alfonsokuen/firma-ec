---
question: "Is the signature legally valid in Ecuador?"
lang: en
order: 3
tags: [legal, lce]
---

Yes, provided your certificate was issued by a **Certification Information Entity (ECI) accredited by ARCOTEL**: BCE, Security Data, ANFAC, Uanataca, Lazzate, Eclipse Soft, Datil. These signatures qualify as **advanced electronic signatures (FEA)** under Ecuador's E-Commerce, Electronic Signatures and Data Messages Law (LCE 2002-67) and have the same legal effect as a handwritten signature (Art. 14).
