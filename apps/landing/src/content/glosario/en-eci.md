---
term: "ECI"
lang: en
acronym: "Entidad de Certificación de Información"
seeAlso: ["arcotel", "ac"]
---

An entity accredited in Ecuador by **ARCOTEL** (Agencia de Regulación y Control de las Telecomunicaciones — Ecuador's telecommunications regulator) to issue digital certificates with legal validity under the LCE. As of 2026 there are 8 active ECIs with roots loaded in firmar.ec: BCE, Security Data, ANFAC, ArgosData, Consejo de la Judicatura (iCert-EC), Uanataca, Eclipse Soft, Datil.
